READY

**Short answer.** Our method already follows Buffett and Munger's *evolved* philosophy, not Graham's cigar-butt method: it buys durable, high-return businesses at a sensible price, uses owner earnings and charges stock compensation. Two rules still reflected views that they themselves revised, and 3.7.0 changes the one that their latest view makes operationally clear:

1. **Management ("$1 test") — changed.** Buffett later wrote that his own retained-dollar test, which compares market value gained with earnings kept, was "improperly formulated" because a falling market premium can fail a business that is still progressing ([Owner's Manual, principle 9](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/ownman.pdf)). Operating companies now pass the management test on **per-share economic progress**, and the market-dollar figure is shown only as context. The tile is renamed **Capital allocation** and charts earnings per share.
2. **Understandable — renamed.** Buffett says the circle of competence belongs to the *investor* ("knowing its boundaries, however, is vital", [1996 letter](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1996.html)). A screen of past margins cannot know what you understand, so the method page and funnel now call it **Earnings resilience**. The rule itself is unchanged.
3. **Discount rate — tested, NOT shipped.** Buffett said he discounts at the long-term government rate without a risk premium and takes his safety from the price ([1996](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/1996/05/06/morning-session---1996-berkshire-hathaway-annual-meeting.html) and [1997](https://buffett.cnbc.com/video/1997/05/05/afternoon-session---1997-berkshire-hathaway-annual-meeting.html) meetings). Applying that literally (the government yield alone, with no +4 points and no 10% floor) breached your veto: U.S. training CAGR −2.78pp and Western ex-U.S. 2016–26 CAGR −3.38pp. The bond+4 / 10% floor and the 15/25/35/50% discounts therefore stay. They are labelled as **our** model choices, not theirs. Buffett also qualified the rule himself: he uses a higher rate when rates are very low, and he paid more for Precision Castparts because rates were near zero.

**GOOGL, MSFT, NVDA, AAPL, KO: no verdict, value or Buy-price change.** All five still pass all five quality tests. None is a Buy, because each is priced above its buy price. Their management pass now rests on per-share earnings growth instead of market value: GOOGL 1.17→8.04, MSFT 2.56→13.64, NVDA 0.066→2.94, AAPL 2.30→6.13, KO 1.49→2.47 (three-year endpoint medians). AAPL used to pass only because its retained earnings were negative (buybacks exceeded profits), so any market gain cleared the $1 bar.

Across the 3,915 published companies, 3.7.0 flips 588 management verdicts on identical inputs (430 fail→pass, 158 pass→fail). In the published output that is 528 quality-code changes: 38 companies become all-five-pass and 2 lose it (e.g. ABBV, whose EPS median fell 3.31→2.41). It also causes 23 compounder-tier switches and 79 published value changes. **Published Buy changes: 0.** Master still has the publication-continuity hold that rules-7 is removing, and rules-7 has not shipped yet, so 49 computed Buy flips are held. **21 of those are new from 3.7.0** (all are new Buys: CHKP, AOS, DOX, WIT, AYI, EFOR, PRG, IMB.LSE, PNDORA.CO, OTEX.TO, EDEN.PA, 2313.HK, five Korean and four Chinese A-shares). They follow the data once rules-7 is on master. No approval step was added.

## 3. What changed in 3.7.0

- `lib/value/tests/management.ts`: the core check is now "per-share economic progress": EPS (book value on the dormant generic financial path) between three-year endpoint medians, at least 7 consecutive observations, ending positive and not lower. The market-dollar comparison no longer decides; it stays as `marketCapGain`/`retainedEarnings` context with `economicProgressBasis: 1`.
- Every surface follows that decision basis. The tile chart, tile numbers, drawer chart/numbers, rule reading, tile reason and the legacy test section only show the retained-dollar window when it *is* the basis. The tile is named **Capital allocation** and charts **Earnings per share** (**Book value per share** for financial companies). The method page, funnel and method-rule copy are renamed accordingly.
- Method 3.7.0 entry and [`changelog.md`](changelog.md) cite sources. Tests are in `tests/unit/value/evolved-principles.test.ts`; existing management/analyze/calibration tests were updated to the new basis.
