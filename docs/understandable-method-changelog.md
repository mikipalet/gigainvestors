# Method candidate changelog

2026-10-05 — method 3.4.0 implemented on value-understand after the frozen numerical gates passed; offline publication proof completed; the broad browser gate is red, so this committed candidate is NOT ready for release. No publication was performed.

For operating companies retain CV <= 0.35. A higher CV passes only with a complete consecutive window of at least seven, up to ten annual observations, all finite strictly positive operating margins and finite nonnegative net income in every year, and either (A) nondecreasing margins with final > first; or (B) OLS margin slope > 0, R-squared >= 0.90, residual population SD / mean margin <= existing 0.35, positive OLS slopes in both nonoverlapping chronological halves (floor(n/2) in first), and latest margin >= previous. Missing observations, losses and sign changes disqualify both exceptions. Flat, falling, erratic, and recently deteriorating paths do not qualify for B. All other checks unchanged. Raw CV retained for valuation/price rules. Financial company rules and provisional-LTM confirmation unchanged.

Only new numerical cutoff: R-squared >= 0.90, chosen from principle: at least nine tenths of observed variation must be explained by a systematic rising trend, no more than one tenth noise. It is not estimated or optimized from returns or current-company examples. Existing 0.35 caps residual CV. Existing seven/ten history limits retained. Half-window and latest-step positive-direction checks prevent a prior rise masking recent deterioration. No return tuning.

Pre-return-evaluation requirement correction: strict monotonicity alone omits the requested trend-consistent improvement. A current-verdict preliminary screen had found zero changes, and an incomplete numeric-only historical replay was running; no candidate portfolio, hit-rate, winner or loser outcomes had been evaluated. Retain draft and disclose both designs; only the following final candidate is evaluated against returns.
