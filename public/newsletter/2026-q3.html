Hoeft Buys $2.18B of Danaher. Mandel Buys $1.1B.

David Hoeft of Dodge & Cox put $2.18B into Danaher (DHR), growing a stake held since 2024 Q1 by 3,821% to $2.23B, now 1.2% of his $186B book. Stephen Mandel made a bigger relative bet: his new $1.1B position is 7.1% of Lone Pine's book and its 2nd-largest holding. Lee Ainslie bought too, while Daniel Loeb sold and Pat Dorsey exited a $119M stake that had been 7% of his book.

David Hoeft's book at the end of 2026 Q3. Green bought, red sold.

Bill Ackman cut Microsoft (MSFT) by 35%, selling $1.1B into a 37% rally from a position opened in 2026 Q1. He started two new positions, $1B of Intercontinental Exchange (ICE) and $954M of Alcon (ALC), each worth about 4 points of impact on Pershing Square's book. His top 5 now holds 61%, the lowest since 2007 Q2.

Meta (META) rose 29% and split the room. Loeb opened a $475M position that is now Third Point's largest holding at 10.7%, and Mandel's new $908M stake ranks 3rd at Lone Pine. Ackman, Terry Smith, Andreas Halvorsen, David Tepper and 4 others sold. Holders still rose from 30 to 33.

Alphabet (GOOGL) fell 4% and drew 11 sellers against 4 buyers. Tepper cut 46% of a stake held since 2014 Q2, Loeb sold 83% of his, and Seth Klarman trimmed a position held since 2020 Q1 by 31%. Smith, Ruane, Coleman, Train and Yongping also sold.

Warren Buffett trimmed Bank of America (BAC) by 6%, selling $1.5B from a position held since 2017 Q3 that remains 8.2% of Berkshire's $304B book. He added 99% to Lennar (LEN), a $1.05B purchase, and put $689M into Bending Spoons (BSP), a new name sitting at 0.2% of the book.

Mohnish Pabrai runs 4 positions worth $362M and traded all of them. He cut Alpha Metallurgical (AMR) by 67% and added 40% to Transocean (RIG), now 41% of the book behind Warrior Met Coal (HCC) at 45%. A Kaspi.kz (KSPI) stake opened last quarter grew 12,192% to $20M.

78 of the 82 active investors have filed, with portfolios worth $1.12T at quarter-end prices against $1.14T last quarter. Francis Chou filed without a single trade across his 30 positions, and Li Lu made 2 trades while Chris Hohn turned over 1% of his book, so all three sit out this letter.
