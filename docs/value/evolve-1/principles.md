# Buffett and Munger: evolution, evidence and rule attribution

Research date: 2026-10-08. Baseline: `6f7e0c1`, method 3.6.0. This document is frozen before implementation or opening candidate return results. Scope is quality, valuation and buying; no holding/selling verdict work.

**The method already reflects their evolved preference for durable earning power; it is not a Graham liquidation-value screen. It is not, however, their personal decision process.** Our financial ratios, fixed cutoffs, automatic verdicts and forecast caps are mostly our inventions. Their latest applicable principles combine business understanding, durable economics, trustworthy capital allocation and a sensible price. They did not publish a universal quantitative stock-selection formula.

“LATEST view” below means the latest applicable position established by these primary sources, not a claim that an old quotation was repeated in every later year. Where a later source qualifies a prior statement, the qualification controls. No inference of a new rule is made merely from a stock appearing in Berkshire's portfolio. The 2025 annual letter is by Greg Abel: it is not new Buffett or Munger testimony.

## Source confidence and counter-evidence

Primary evidence supports the qualitative evolution with high confidence. Berkshire hosts original letters/manual/Wesco; Columbia publishes Buffett's own essay; Stripe Press publishes Munger's authorised speech; CNBC's archive provides direct meeting testimony. CNBC full-page retrieval was intermittently unavailable, so indexed transcript excerpts were cross-checked with targeted searches; this limits surrounding-context verification. P16 quotes Buffett’s direct statement, not the chapter heading summarizing Munger. No numerical floor is inferred from it.

Latest does not mean most permissive. Buffett still preferred high-return, low-capital businesses after buying utilities. His utility warnings became stronger in 2023. Neither Apple nor Google regret repeals valuation discipline. Price discounts and opportunity costs are complementary; our existing price and IRR gates are mathematically overlapping when they use the same cash-flow model and rate. Counting them as two independent tests of safety overstates their independence.

There is no primary-source prescription for our 15/25/35/50% price discounts, five quality tests, standardised 10% hurdle, compounder thresholds, median windows or growth caps. The historically documented Berkshire $1 test was itself revised. A screen can implement evidence-based approximations without claiming to reproduce personal judgment.

## Frozen candidate specification and decision rule

No candidate return outcomes have been opened. Two independent numerical changes and their combination will be evaluated; presentation clarification has no standalone numerical effect.

1. **`government_discount`**: set the operating/financial central intrinsic discount rate to the already supplied finite government bond yield. Remove +4pp and10% floor from intrinsic discounting only. Retain legacy rate separately as `requiredReturn`, explicitly a model opportunity-cost proxy. Preserve all current cash flows, growth estimates, safety tiers, share/FX inputs and price correctness. Missing rate or a rate at/below terminal growth (operating) or perpetual growth (financial) means no comparable computed value; never insert epsilon, negative infinite value, or an unregistered floor. The highest acceptable price must be no higher than both intrinsic value after MOS and the price delivering the separate required return. No NAV change: that path has no bond input. P11/P12 support the rate separation; P11/P16 caution that low-rate judgment remains unimplemented. This is a testable approximation, not a claim that Buffett always used the current10y rate.
2. **`economic_progress`**: in the operating management test, use the existing complete-consecutive per-share earnings progression (positive ending three-year median, nonnegative change, ≥7 observations) regardless of market-cap-price availability. Keep original market dollar/retention metrics and clearly label them historical context. No new number, no benchmark fabricated, no financial book test change. P06/P19 identify the false market-rerating verdict mechanism; this closest existing proxy is explicitly N, not Buffett's corrected two-part test. It can miss poor reinvestment that still leaves EPS rising; existing acquisition/dilution checks remain.
3. **`combined`** applies both. If an individual is rejected, evaluate the remaining accepted combination without tuning parameters. Presentation names and assumptions must reflect the actual shipped arm.

All other N/O rows have their lack of a determinate replacement and closest retained rule stated above. No 15%→11% capital-return shortcut, tech exception, arbitrary forecast acceleration, reduced safety tiers, invented maintenance inputs, or outcome-selected parameter search.

Use research-1/rules-5 fixed retrospective cohort/portfolio definitions: US2005–2015 training; Western ex-US and all ex-US both periods; US2016–2026 **CONTAMINATED**. Report absolute CAGR, max drawdown, changes, three-year hit rates, new buys, loser IDs and missing labels. A change is **NOT for shipping if any required US-training/international panel loses more than2pp CAGR or worsens drawdown more than5pp**. Exactly2/5pp is allowed.
