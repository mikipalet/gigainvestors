# Phase 1: complete decision-rule inventory (baseline 78141da, method 3.4.0)

This inventory describes the executable method, including paths not shown on a typical operating-company page. It precedes candidate implementation. The numeric cutoffs are this project's operational choices, **not quotations or numbers prescribed by Buffett or Munger**. The principles below paraphrase their emphasis on durable earning power, owner cash, per-share capital allocation and price discipline: [1986 owner earnings and capital allocation](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1986.html), [1992 value and growth](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/1992.html), [2007 durable economics](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/letters/2007ltr.pdf).

Evidence conventions: **A** = single-year sensitivity, **B** = improvement treated like deterioration, **D** = operating/financial inconsistency. These identify mathematical exposure, not established misclassification. **E/C** empirical evidence is in `outputs/audit-current-10y.csv`, `audit-ranked-rules.csv`, `audit-subtest-predictiveness.csv` and `audit-losing-quality-passers.csv`. Every observed subtest has pass/fail/unknown denominators and quarter-weighted three-year hit rates, split US 2005–2015, contaminated later US, Western non-US and all non-US in both periods. Thresholds not crossed or not stored cannot have a measured pass/fail contrast; flags that do not determine verdicts have no independent historical verdict test. Unknown is not pass.

## Coverage, routing and composition

| Rule / threshold | Measures and one-sentence principle | Failure exposure / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum seven distinct fiscal years; fewer overrides all five numeric/results to unclear, even an apparent fail | Enough evidence to judge a record: avoid claiming certainty from a short history. | Young eventual winners necessarily excluded; not evidence for weakening history; current and cohort coverage disclosed. |
| Most quality windows use last ten fiscal years; metrics usually need ≥5 observations; chronological gaps vary by subtest | A cycle of evidence: evaluate durable rather than one-period performance. | Missing observations can make optional checks disappear; cannot infer safety from missing data. |
| Historical snapshots require ≥10 distinct available annual years, versus seven in current quality | Historical replay coverage: use the evidence the actual harness requires. | D-like current/history inconsistency and young-company selection bias; retained unchanged to preserve measurement. |
| Any decisive failure or ≥minimum failures wins; otherwise missing core → unclear; known core checks allow pass despite optional missing; without core require ≥2/3 available | Fail on demonstrated weakness, distinguish ignorance from proof. | Optional missing risks can permit financial passes; applies also to operating optional tests. |
| Bank/insurer dispatch replaces all five operating tests; lending assets/assets ≥40% in classification; AXP exception only with loan field absent | Match measurements to the business model. | D: model suitability is necessary, equivalent difficulty is not established. Financial returns are not directly comparable to operating ROIC. |
| LTM: four consecutive quarters (~91.3125 days ±20), filed before cutoff, reconciled split/currency fields; each test requires its inputs; provisional share ratios <1.5 relative to annual | Respond to new information without mistaking an incomplete quarter for a year. | Latest incomplete fields restore annual test; changed verdict needs two consecutive quarters; annual changes immediate. A reduces noise but two observations are overlapping. |
| Snapshot filing strictly before quarter-end; missing filing date +90 days; no current share override | Do not act on information unavailable at the signal. | Restatements, current classifications/bond yields/FX and retrospective judgement remain; not pristine point-in-time data. |
| All five quality tests must pass, integrity must pass, price must pass, no business-changed veto | A wonderful business still requires a sensible purchase price. | Conjunction rejects many winners; ranking individual failures is overlapping, not additive. |
| Integrity: unexplained share ratio >5× or <0.2×; split factor tolerance 3%, corroborating price 20%; balance discrepancy >10% in ≥2 of last five years | Verify the units before trusting the calculation. | A can hold an anomalous filing; data repair, not quality relaxation, is the remedy. |

## Operating companies: five tests and every subtest

| Test / rule | Measures and principle | Failure exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Understandable: ≥7 years; revenue changes need ≥5 valid consecutive pairs, at most five declines over ten years (11 baseline observations) | Demand resilience: prefer an understandable recurring revenue record. | One decline does not fail; B no, growth never counted as decline; quantity ignores decline severity. |
| At most two net-loss years, at least five income observations | Persistent profitability: prefer businesses that earn through a cycle. | One loss already allowed; three tiny losses can fail where one large loss passes. |
| Mean operating margin ≥0, at least five margins | Positive operating economics: a business must earn more than its operating costs. | A: severe loss can outweigh positives; negative average is also substantive evidence. |
| Population operating-margin CV ≤0.35; denominator absolute mean; mean zero CV unavailable | Predictability: a dependable record should support a forecast. | A/B clear: one trough or a rising level inflates dispersion; NVDA example. |
| 3.4.0 exception: complete consecutive 7–10 years, every margin >0 and net income ≥0; nondecreasing and ends higher OR OLS slope >0, R²≥0.90, residual CV≤0.35, positive slopes in both nonoverlapping halves, no latest decline | Sustained improvement is not deterioration. | B partly corrected; A a recovered trough breaks monotonicity and trend R²; no exception for losses, gaps or missing values. |
| Moat: median ROIC ≥15%, ≥5 returns; second-lowest ≥10% (one bad year allowed) | Durable advantage earns high returns on capital over a record. | Already robust to one outlier; two old weak years can reject an improving company. |
| NOPAT uses operating earnings ×(1−tax), tax bounded 0–35%, default 21%; invested capital = equity+debt+extra leases−cash−goodwill | Evaluate economic earning power on required capital. | Acquisitions/goodwill exclusion can inflate returns; capital fallback uses equity+debt+leases; no-positive-capital positive-profit sentinel 100.0001%. Missing goodwill can only prove a pass via conservative capital floor. |
| Gross-margin decline ≤4pp: FY2023 vs mean(FY2019,FY2020), regardless of evaluation date | Pricing power should survive adversity. | **A/D/E clear**: a fixed historical trough survives full recovery; before FY2023 the optional check is unavailable and therefore cannot constrain pass; no analogous financial margin veto. |
| Economics: five-year summed owner earnings / net income ≥0.8, all five values present; nonpositive aggregate NI passes only when aggregate owner cash >0 | Owners need distributable cash, not paper profit. | Sums already average cycles; A a huge outlier can matter, but cash investment is a real cost. Minority uncertainty uses consolidated OCF−all capex−SBC−leases vs consolidated income. |
| ROIIC ≥12%, at least seven consecutive years within last 11; endpoint NOPAT increase / cumulative capex−D&A+ΔNWC; fallback Δinvested capital | New money retained should earn an attractive return. | A: endpoints dominate, acquisitions distort capital; capital release with increasing earnings passes, declining earnings does not; unchanged. |
| Working capital/revenue: last three mean−first three mean ≤10pp OR last mean≤0; full ten years and six endpoints needed | Growth should not swallow disproportionate working cash. | Three-year averaging protects one year; improvement never fails; optional missing check. |
| Management: market-cap gain ≥sum retained earnings over earliest priced complete 7–10-year interval | Retained profits should create at least equivalent owner value. | A endpoints and market mood matter; uses aggregate market cap (issuance risk), with dilution separately tested. |
| When market prices unavailable: latest contiguous ≥7 per-share annual observations, medians of first/last three; end >0 and change≥0 | Evaluate what each share owns when market prices cannot settle value creation. | D market gain vs fundamental per-share fallback are different tests; medians resist one outlier. Missing prices may be pending, missing filings may not. |
| Ordinary dilution CAGR ≤1% in **either** ten years (11 observations) or five years (six); both available and both above →fail, incomplete otherwise unclear | Each owner must participate in growth. | A acquisition issuance may affect endpoints; evidence-backed acquisition shares excluded only here; actual shares still govern valuation; financial threshold 2% and crisis exclusions differ. Either-window rule can hide recent dilution. |
| Buyback timing fails only with ≥6 positive-buyback years, Spearman rho<−0.5 vs earnings yield, mean buyback yield>1% | Repurchases should buy value, not merely reduce share count. | Correlation imperfect valuation proxy; single outlier mitigated by ranks; debt-funded buybacks informational only. |
| Acquisition spending >50% of ten-year cumulative NI fails only if last-three median ROIC <15% **and** <2/3 first-three median | Acquisitions must earn their cost. | Requires repeated deterioration, not one acquisition year; missing 10-year amounts optional; goodwill/intangibles additions proxy can omit cash deals. |
| Accounting Sloan (NI−OCF)/assets ≤10%, latest year, core | Earnings should be supported by cash. | A one working-capital shock; accruals alone not enough to fail (two-flag rule). |
| Latest receivables/sales ratio divided by prior ratio ≤1.465 | Sales should collect, not accumulate as receivables. | A endpoint/acquisition/mix effects; only combines with another flag. |
| Nonzero restructuring/one-time charges in ≤2 of last five years; all five present | Repeated exceptional charges are recurring economics. | Allows one charge; any nonzero amount counts, no materiality floor; missing optional. |
| Latest SBC/OCF ≤15% | Share compensation is a real owner cost. | A denominator trough, and negative OCF yields unavailable ratio; decisive cash test covers positive NI/negative OCF. |
| Fail on ≥2 accounting flags; **decisive** latest OCF≤0 with NI>0 fails alone | Profits without cash need a credible explanation. | A severe single-year sensitivity; financial path has no OCF rule because financial cash flow is not comparable. Do not waive actual cash trouble on fame alone. |
| Goodwill+intangibles/equity, debt-funded buybacks, NWC slope, ROIC fallback-year count | Context for the owner, not additional verdict hurdles. | Informational values must not be counted as failed subtests. |

## Banks and insurers (actual financial.ts path)

`config.moat.roeMedianFin=12%` and `roeSecondLowestFin=8%` exist in the generic moat function but **are not the dispatched bank/insurer rule**. Financial.ts actually uses the following; confusing dormant constants with active thresholds misstates the method.

| Rule | Measures and principle | Failure exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Complete positive-share/equity suffix of up to 11 consecutive years; seven minimum | Judge financial capital through a cycle. | Missing fields block compounding, not always optional accounting checks. |
| Understandable: strictly positive common NI in ceil(90%×years) for banks, ceil(80%×years) insurers | Financial promises require resilient earnings. | At ten years banks allow one nonpositive year, insurers two; operating permits two negative years (zero differs). **ROE CV informational**, D. |
| Common book = equity−preferred; tangible common book also subtracts goodwill/intangibles (missing treated zero here) | Common owners bear residual capital risk. | D versus operating missing-goodwill floor; undisclosed preferred/common earnings use disclosed parent-total proxy with caveat. |
| Moat common capital latest >0, core | Capital must remain available to absorb losses. | A latest impairment can fail; prudent loss absorption constraint, not evidence of false failure. |
| Banks/P&C return on tangible common book median ≥12%, other insurers common ROE median ≥10%; ≥5 returns | Financial franchise should earn adequately on owner capital. | D versus operating 15% ROIC, different leverage. Capital≤0 becomes zero return. |
| Bank second-lowest tangible common return ≥5% | A bank should withstand more than one weak year. | Already permits one bad year; insurers have no worst-return veto. |
| Bank median efficiency ratio≤65%, ≥5 observations; missing expense/revenue ratio falls back when available | Efficient intermediation protects owners. | Median robust; missing optional can allow easy pass. |
| P&C/reinsurance: ≥7 combined-ratio<100% years; usable when 7 successes or >3 observed failures, otherwise return-based fallback | Insurance float is valuable when underwriting earns or costs little. | D seven-year absolute requirement across short histories, fallback can pass unprofitable underwriting; no combined ratios for many insurers. |
| Insurer float CAGR≥0 when endpoints and complete history available | Valuable float should persist. | Endpoint-sensitive A; deliberate runoff may fail although economic; no evidence that every runoff is strong. |
| Economics: chained annual (book/share+common dividend/share)/prior book/share CAGR ≥7%; all book positive, ≥7 consecutive observations | Financial value should compound per share including distributions. | Dividends reinvested at book; any nonpositive book fails; D versus operating cash/ROIIC tests. |
| Management ordinary share CAGR≤2%; exclude >10% annual share jumps specifically in 2008/2009; full CAGR still displayed | Recapitalization is different from routine dilution, but per-share value must survive. | **A/D** arbitrary calendar exception and looser operating threshold; crisis issuance still economically dilutive. |
| Book/share gain ≥retained common earnings/share, subtracting dividends and buybacks; retained≤0 needs book gain≥0; floating tolerance 1e−9×max(1,abs retained) | Retention must create owner capital. | Endpoint A, book cost basis can punish productive intangibles; unreported buybacks zero (conservative). |
| Accounting latest common book>0; no reported restatement in last ten | Financial capital and accounts must be credible. | A any restatement, no severity distinction; absence unreported explicitly caveated but passes known-restatement test. |
| Whole-window loan CAGR ≤2×max(0,deposit CAGR), or nonpositive loan growth | Credit expansion should have durable funding. | D alternative funding models; optional endpoint sensitivity. |
| Credit provision/loans exceeds same-year peers in fewer than ceil(60%×paired years), at least five pairs | Repeated abnormal losses can expose weak underwriting. | Peer risk mix imperfect; conservative provisioning can look worse; missing data removes check. |
| Adverse reserve development>0 in fewer than three years, at least five observations | Underwriting profits should not be borrowed from future reserve corrections. | Already permits isolated year; magnitude ignored; missing data removes check. |
| Financial accounting fails on one known failed check, unlike operating two flags | Financial capital demands careful accounts. | D not automatically more lenient: nonpositive capital/restatement is immediate failure. Loan-growth excess-year count informational, not separate veto. |

## Valuation and price (not changed by quality-rule experiments)

| Rule/input | Measures and principle | Failure exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Owner earnings: NI + allocated D&A−maintenance−SBC−lease cash, or OCF−all capex when D&A/growth estimate unavailable; allocate consolidated adjustments to parent | Value cash an owner can take without injuring the business. | Growth investment estimates uncertain; expensed SBC remains an owner cost; missing optional SBC defaults only with complete statement, otherwise disclosed. |
| Growth capex = positive Δrevenue×mean trailing five PPE/revenue; maintenance=max(capex−growth capex,min(capex,D&A)); evidence override floored at D&A | Separate upkeep from expansion conservatively. | Improvement may be undercounted; not symmetric deterioration penalty; acquisition proxy uncertainty. |
| Lease cash as reported; incomplete disqualifies; lease depreciation with lease liabilities and no cash figure estimates 20% liabilities | A lease is a cash obligation. | Estimate may distort individual years; cannot remove economic cost without evidence. |
| Normalize five complete recent owner-earnings years to min(median,latest,newer usable TTM); must be >0 | Do not capitalize a transient peak. | **A/B** latest trough dominates, rising business lags median; deliberate conservative asymmetric cap. |
| Discount max(10%,local 10-year government yield+4pp); missing yield no value (except NAV) | Value future cash at a demanding required return. | Current yields in historical replay not vintage; no candidate tuning. |
| Growth=min(finite per-share owner earnings CAGR,revenue CAGR,organic revenue proxy,ROIIC×reinvestment); clamp 0–8%, zero if none | Growth must be supported by economics and capital. | Endpoint A; minimum pessimism underestimates transitions; 11 observations for decade CAGR. |
| Organic growth subtracts acquisitions/positive asset additions share, capped at 100%; reinvestment=sum(capex−D&A+ΔNWC)/NOPAT | Do not label purchased earnings organic compounding. | Acquisition proxy and endpoints can dominate; research-1 valuation diagnostics quantify limited winner rescue. |
| Latest annual or complete newer TTM revenue below three years prior →0 growth | Do not project growth into a shrinking record. | A latest trough; no rolling smoothing; predictable contraction is economically relevant. |
| Standard growth five years then fade to 3% by year ten; perpetual terminal 3% | Future growth must converge to a sustainable rate. | Forecast uncertainty; not a demonstrated a/b/d/e code defect. |
| Compounder requires all quality pass, noncyclical, ≥8 annual ROIC with median≥20%, ≥8 owner-earnings/all-capital returns with median≥15% | High growth deserves credit only with durable returns on all money paid. | Includes goodwill/acquired intangibles; CV-based cyclical status can still penalize improvement after quality pass. |
| Compounder growth: 11 positive consecutive per-share owner-earnings years; ten log changes winsorized one at each tail; cap12%, ten-year fade to3%; base MOS15% | Smooth unusually strong compounding without extrapolating extremes. | Already robust to one growth outlier, but any negative year disqualifies; no license to bypass price. |
| Add excess cash=max(0,cash−2% revenue)×parent share; no double subtraction of debt from after-interest owner cash | Count cash once and obligations coherently. | Annual balances may lag acquisition commitments: WBD 2018 documented in research-1. |
| Net debt/normalized OE >3 years →moderate, >5 →volatile; MOS max(base,35%/50%) | Leverage requires a larger cushion. | A normalization trough increases leverage; real debt remains. |
| Financial value: positive tangible book/share; ≥5 finite tangible returns, median ROE clamped0–25%; ≥5 dividend retentions clamped0–1; growth=ROE×median retention capped6%, shrinkage→0 | Capitalize sustainable distributable financial earnings. | D quality common capital basis versus valuation parent tangible basis; insurers often common book in quality yet tangible book in value. |
| Financial distributable return=min(ROE−g,4×(r−g)); multiple=payout/(r−g), mid effectively capped4× book; r>g | Growth consumes retained capital; do not pay unlimited multiples of book. | Protective cap, no direct causal predictive evidence. |
| Low/high operating scenarios growth/2 and r+1pp vs same growth and r−min(1pp,(r−3%)/2); financial low/high r± analogous | Show estimation uncertainty. | Range is scenario output, buy uses mid only. |
| NAV holdings: explicit named list excluding Berkshire, or asset manager/capital markets with five consecutive years fair-value gains>50% total income | An investment portfolio is valued through its assets and owner returns. | Classification exception; no invented consolidated-equity NAV. |
| NAV needs ≥7 consecutive same-currency positive NAV/share and nonnegative known dividends; annual reinvestment factors; growth capped12%; discount ten-year NAV realization10% | Value what a portfolio can deliver without double-counting dividends. | Endpoint/missing history; no cash/debt added twice. |
| MOS stable25% (CV≤0.20), moderate35% (CV≤0.35), volatile50% (above, missing or commodity); compounder15%; NAV price≤85% both NAV and discounted realization | Leave room for error in price, proportionate to uncertainty. | **B** raw CV still treats rising margins volatile; retained to avoid coupling quality relief with a cheaper price hurdle. |
| Price ≤mid×(1−MOS) →pass; insufficient discount but price≤mid →unclear; price>mid →fail | A fine business at an excessive price is not a buy. | Misses expensive future winners by design, research-1 separates quality and price failures. |
| Expected IRR of exactly same cash-flow model≥discount rate (10% floor), tolerance1e−12; cash already covering purchase with future nonnegative cash qualifies | Every buy must promise sufficient modeled owner return. | Usually redundant with positive MOS, not two independent causes of all missed winners. |
| Shares: positive annual count; current Yahoo fallback only with missing annual; replace if >1.5× discrepancy or >10% with post-year split evidence | Per-share value requires the correct denominator. | Unit corrections not economic relaxation; independent two-source counts agree within2%, refreshed≤7days; NAV not redenominated. |
| Unverified price/value outside0.2×–20× or market cap vs price×shares discrepancy>2% →review; verified shares can clear | Implausible bargains need independent evidence. | Possible extreme winner false holds; measurement safety gate not quality verdict. |
| Frozen price/quality records and changed-business veto are preserved by publication | A stored owner decision must remain attributable. | Freeze can differ from current calculations; enumerate rather than silently rewrite. |

## Accounting/business flags outside the five verdicts

These are evidence/display rules, **not automatic quality or BUY vetoes**. Their principle is to expose owner-relevant obligations, incentives and accounting choices. Historical dated flag panels are unavailable; independent pass/fail prediction is therefore **not measured**, not zero.

| Flag / threshold | Interpretation and single-year/symmetric risks |
|---|---|
| Capex/D&A>1 context; red only >2, rising over three consecutive years, positive capex growth and slower owner-earnings growth | Already avoids treating productive expansion alone as bad; earnings endpoint A remains. |
| Net cash>0 with debt≥0 | Financial room, not a quality guarantee. |
| Goodwill/assets>50% | Large acquisition premiums; old goodwill/equity flag suppressed; does not fail sound acquisitions by itself. |
| Shares YoY>2% | Ordinary/acquisition/split distinctions need evidence; informational D vs1%/2% CAGR tests. |
| Receivables/inventory growth>10%, >sales growth+10pp, amount≥2% sales | Collection/inventory pressure, but expansion/acquisition year can trigger A. |
| Explicit nonoverlapping purchase+lease+uncommenced commitments>2×OE | Fixed cash claims; no summing missing obligations. |
| Positive uncommenced leases; guarantees+credit derivatives>0; debt due2y>cash; pension deficit>0 | Evidence of future cash claims, not their expected losses. |
| Adjusted/reported profit>1.2 with reported>0, or adjusted positive while reported≤0 | Omitted recurring costs; genuine one-off year A possible. |
| Buyback price differs >10% from contemporaneous model value | Rewards cheap repurchases; model error possible. |
| Insider ownership>10% and≤100%; dividend-increase streak20–150 years; inflation>2% plus price increases and maintained GM | Alignment and pricing/distribution strengths, not predictive vetoes. |
| Customer/supplier concentration≥10%,≤100%, group rather than subsidiary revenue basis | Concentrated dependency; does not alone imply weakness. |
| Monetary flag shown only exposure>2% market cap OR>10% annual OE, same currency; severity80+19×size/(size+4) | Materiality matters more than absolute amount; small-denominator A can elevate ranking. |
| Related parties, guarantees, vendor finance, credit backstops, residual guarantees, asset-life changes, actual unremediated material weaknesses and auditor changes require dated filing evidence | Read concrete disclosures, not boilerplate; no numeric independent verdict or historical rate available. |
| Extraction trust: accuracy≥90%, n≥10, ≥3 positive/negative; per-class≥90%; evidence URL/date/quote; confidence≥90% or reviewed; quantity≥90% or reviewed | An unsupported reading must not create an investment conclusion. |
| Numeric judgement trust≥90%, n≥10, ≥3 positive/negative, reading confidence≥70%, current question version; capex override≥85%; current report applies at most three annual periods | Evidence may correct inputs but cannot override the resulting numeric verdict; one-off charge must be year-specific reported amount; acquisition issuance weighted-average contribution. |
| Jev contradiction probability≥70%, evidence/trust checks; bank government dependence excluded; commodity probability60%; evidence60%, trust agreement85% | Readings explain risk; combine() returns numeric verdict, no separate qualitative pass override. |

No blanket removal of accounting, capital-return, share or financial-risk checks follows from weak return correlations. A quality measure can be economically sensible without predicting price returns, particularly in a survivor-biased panel. Numeric severity, loss of capital and future stock returns are different targets.

### Input validation details that also bound values

Bank routing uses **strictly greater than** 40% lending/client assets, not ≥40%. MCO and insurance brokers stay operating; insurer SIC is 6310≤SIC<6400; bank industry names route bank; capital-market companies with deposits route bank; broker cash is inferred client assets only when >3×equity. Remaining insurance industries route insurer.

Bond input sanity bands (fractions) are CH −1.5%–4%, JP −1%–6%, CN/TW 0–8%, BR/ZA 0–25%, MX/ID 0–20%, EG/NG/GH/KE/UG/ZM/TZ/MW 0–40%, PK/LK 0–35%, AR 0–100%, other −1.5%–15%. Latest daily observation must be ≤7 days old; 30-day median requires ≥5 distinct dates; a move greater than max(1pp,50% of median) uses a sane median. US independent treasury disagreement>0.5pp uses the second source; IE ECB monthly fallback≤62 days; CH emergency documented default1%. No bond acquisition is run in this audit. These data-quality rules cannot be independently ranked from the frozen research panel. Cached prices, split basis and FX remain identical across comparisons.
