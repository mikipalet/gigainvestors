Buffett adds $17B of Alphabet as 15 others sell

Warren Buffett bought $17.2B of Alphabet (GOOGL) in the quarter, lifting the stake 45% to $37.8B and making it 12.6% of Berkshire Hathaway's book, its third-largest position. Berkshire first bought it in 2025 Q3. The stock rose 24% over the quarter, and Buffett was one of only 4 buyers on the site; 15 investors sold, among them Andreas Halvorsen, who exited a $689M stake entirely. Alphabet is now held by 49 of the 81 filers, down from 52.

Warren Buffett's book at the end of 2026 Q2. Green bought, red sold.

Everything else at Berkshire was small by comparison. Buffett cut Bank of America (BAC) by 6%, or $1.7B, a position held since 2017 Q3, and trimmed Capital One (COF) by 58% and Nucor (NUE) by 52%, both down to under 1% of the book. Delta Air Lines (DAL), first bought in 2026 Q1, grew 44% to $5.4B. Turnover was 8%, and the top five holdings now make up 72% of the book, the highest since 2025 Q1.

Bill Ackman opened four positions at once: Visa (V) at $1.12B, Mastercard (MA) at $1.09B, S&P Global (SPGI) at $1.06B and Netflix (NFLX) at $934M, each between 5% and 6% of a 14-stock book. He cut Amazon (AMZN) by 25% and added 48% to Howard Hughes (HHH), held since 2010 Q4 and now 10.2% of the book. Pershing Square's top five fell from 78% to 58%, the lowest concentration since 2007 Q2.

Terry Smith turned over 66% of Fundsmith and sold his entire $717M stake in Mettler-Toledo (MTD). In came Uber (UBER) at $645M, Mastercard at $639M, Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM) at $548M and Netflix at $499M. His top five now account for 30% of the book, the lowest since 2018 Q2. Smith was also among the Alphabet sellers.

Daniel Loeb's book went from $2.1B to $4.6B. His new Warner Bros. Discovery (WBD) position, at $533M, is now his largest holding at 12% of assets, and he raised Alphabet by 486% and Norfolk Southern (NSC) by 500%. Stephen Mandel turned over 88% of Lone Pine, selling Vistra (VST) for $930M and opening Yandex (YNDX) at $1.18B as his top position, plus Seagate (STX), Home Depot (HD), Applied Materials (AMAT) and Linde (LIN).

Prem Watsa's two largest holdings are now gold miners. He added 84% to Orla Mining (ORLA), held since 2022 Q3 and now 22% of Fairfax's book, and opened Eldorado Gold (EGO) at $427M, or 16%. He also raised Kraft Heinz (KHC) by 172%. Li Lu added 134% to Pinduoduo (PDD), taking it to 22% of a 7-stock book, and exited Bank of America, Occidental (OXY), S&P Global and H&R Block; his top five are 98% of assets, the highest since 2024 Q2.

81 investors filed, holding $1.15T against $1.06T last quarter. Michael Burry has not filed since 2025 Q3. William Von Mueffling's filing cuts every position by the same amount, which reads as a reporting change, so it is left out. SpaceX (SPCX) appears in 5 filings, up from none.
